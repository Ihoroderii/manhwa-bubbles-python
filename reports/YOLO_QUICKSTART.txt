┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  💡 WHAT IT DOES:                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Load your manga image
2. Use YOLO AI to detect all people/characters
3. Estimate head positions
4. Automatically place speech bubbles above heads
5. Add your dialogue text
6. Save the final image

All automatic! 🎉

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚙️  CONFIGURATION:                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Adjust detection sensitivity:
  confidence=0.15  # Detect more people (more sensitive)
  confidence=0.25  # Default (balanced)
  confidence=0.50  # Detect fewer people (conservative)

Choose YOLO model:
  'yolov8n.pt'  # Fast, 6MB (recommended)
  'yolov8s.pt'  # Better, 22MB
  'yolov8m.pt'  # Best, 52MB

Adjust bubble position:
  bubble_offset_y=150  # Closer to head
  bubble_offset_y=200  # Default
  bubble_offset_y=300  # Further from head


┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🎯 WHAT YOLO DETECTS:                                       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

✅ Works well with:
  - Realistic manga art
  - Semi-realistic characters
  - Clear human figures
  - Full body or upper body shots

⚠️  May struggle with:
  - Very stylized/chibi art
  - Extreme close-ups
  - Abstract styles
  
  → Use manual click method for difficult styles


┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING:                                         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Problem: No people detected
Solution: Lower confidence to 0.15-0.20

Problem: Too many false detections
Solution: Increase confidence to 0.40-0.50

Problem: Wrong bubble positions
Solution: Adjust bubble_offset_y (150-300)
